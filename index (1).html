FAQ

Quick answers

How do I stick it on a wall or shelf?

Peel the backing from the magnetic base, press it onto a clean, dry surface for a few seconds, then snap the light bar on.

How do I change the brightness?

Tap the bar once to turn it on or off. Press and hold to move smoothly between dim and bright, then release where you like it.

How do I charge it?

Take the bar off the base and plug in the USB cable. Any phone or laptop charger works.

Can I use it as a torch?

Yes. Detach the bar and carry it. It works as a slim emergency light.
